Lattice Occupational Pensions Taxonomy — 2025 change memo
Status: approved by the taxonomy board, 12 Jan 2025
From: taxonomy board
To: filing desk / DTS maintainers

This is the approved delta from the 2024 DTS. It is not a linkbase. The 2024
definition linkbase remains the last accepted DRS for that year; it is not
the 2025 DRS.

Scheme type
Occupational is the default scheme type from 2025. Personal stays a reportable
member. Hybrid is a grouping heading only — Cash Balance and Mixed Benefit sit
under it and are the reportable hybrid members. Do not report the heading.

Geography
Domestic remains the default. Overseas is a grouping heading only; EEA and
Rest of World are the reportable members under it. The leftover "Other"
geography member from 2024 is withdrawn.

Member class
Active, Deferred and Pensioner are in the flow cube. There is no default
member class — if a fact uses the class dimension it has to name a real
member. Dependant is in the schema for leftover tagging and is not in the
2025 domain.

Currency (administration expenses only)
Presentation currency remains the default. Settlement currency is reportable.
The extension member ext:Other is the only "Other" that belongs on this
dimension. Do not treat a same-named leftover in the core namespace as a
currency member.

Line item cubes
Net assets keep a scheme cube. The flow lines (change in net assets and
everything under it) keep a flow cube covering scheme, geography and member
class.

Contributions (and employer contributions under them) pick up an exclusion
cube: they must not be tagged to Pensioner on that cube's side. That
exclusion is not a positive cube. It is an open exclusion — Pensioner stays
forbidden even if other dimensions appear on that same context side.

Benefits paid pick up an exclusion cube for the combination Personal + Rest
of World. That exclusion is also open: Personal + Rest of World stays
forbidden even when another valid flow dimension (for example member class)
is present on the same side.

Both of those notAll has-hypercube relationships are open exclusions
(xbrldt:closed="false") so an extra dimension cannot switch them off.

Administration expenses pick up a currency cube, in addition to the flow
cube on the movement lines.

What was not approved
Reinstating the 2024 scheme default. Putting member class on the scheme cube.
Leaving Overseas or Hybrid reportable. A member-class default. Keeping the
2024 geography Other member. Moving the contributions exclusion onto the
wrong context side. An open admin cube. Hanging employer contributions on
the scheme cube. Dropping the benefits-paid exclusion.
